The rules, in full.

Everything here is enforced by the Sisonke contract on Stellar. The app only offers what the contract accepts, and the contract refuses the rest, even if someone skips the app entirely.

What the contract refuses

The transaction fails and the money stays exactly where it was.

  • A release signed by anyone except the payer.
  • A refund signed by anyone except the recipient.
  • A second deposit into an escrow that’s already funded.
  • Any release, refund or reclaim once it’s Released or Refunded.
  • A release or refund before anything is deposited.
  • A reclaim before the deadline, or a deposit after it.
  • An escrow for zero USDC, or one where you pay yourself.

No back door

No admin function can move, freeze or redirect money in an escrow. The website holds no keys. If it disappeared tomorrow, every escrow would still work from Stellar Lab.

Status follows the chain

The app changes status only after Stellar confirms. If a transaction fails, the escrow stays exactly as it was and the app says why.

Why the payer can reclaim

It’s the one time someone can send money to themselves. Without it, money would be stuck forever if the recipient vanished. It only works after the deadline both people saw at the start.
